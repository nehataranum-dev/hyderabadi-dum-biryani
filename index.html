HYDERABADI DUM BIRYANI

Pride of Hyderabad

Hyderabadi Dum Biryani is more than just a delicious meal; it is a part of Hyderabad's culture and identity. Fragrant basmati rice, tender meat, aromatic spices, fried onions, saffron and fresh herbs come together to create a dish loved far beyond Hyderabad.

What Makes It Special?

The traditional dum cooking method is what makes Hyderabadi biryani so special. The pot is sealed and the ingredients slowly cook together, allowing the rice, meat and spices to develop a rich aroma and flavour.

Ingredients

  • Basmati rice
  • Chicken or mutton
  • Onions
  • Yogurt
  • Ginger garlic paste
  • Green chillies
  • Whole spices
  • Biryani spices
  • Saffron
  • Fresh coriander and mint

How Is It Made?

  1. Marinate the meat with yogurt, spices and herbs.
  2. Prepare the basmati rice until it is partly cooked.
  3. Layer the marinated meat and rice in a cooking pot.
  4. Add fried onions, saffron and fresh herbs.
  5. Seal the pot and cook everything slowly on dum.

Why I Love Biryani

For me, biryani is not simply food. It reminds me of Hyderabad, family gatherings and celebrations. There is something special about opening a pot of freshly cooked biryani and smelling the spices and saffron. Every family has its own way of preparing it, which makes the dish even more personal and special.
